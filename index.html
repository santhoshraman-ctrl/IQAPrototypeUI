How iQA works

iQA identifies campaign configuration errors before activation, so avoidable write-offs are prevented.

Five steps

  1. Campaign. Connect SharePoint, choose a campaign, then confirm its details and files.
  2. Validation. iQA checks what is configured in the ad platform against the plan, line item by line item.
  3. Review. You confirm each finding, or mark it as not an issue.
  4. Fix & re-validate. Confirmed fixes are exported to the Programmatic Team. Re-run validation until every line item matches.
  5. Write-off summary. See the write-off prevented, then download the report.

Terms

  • Write-off. Media spend that is wasted, or not delivered where planned, because of a configuration error.
  • Write-off at risk. The part of a line item’s budget an error affects. Each figure shows its own calculation under “See the math”.
  • Finding. One difference between the plan and the ad platform.
